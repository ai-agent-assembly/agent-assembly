## Efficiency evidence

Per AAASM-5831's AC — a compact comparison against the old giant-prompt/full-
sweep approach, not precise token accounting:

- **Shared discovery performed once, reused by every worker.** The
  verification manifest and risk map were each generated exactly once at the
  merged HEAD and handed to all 3 parallel dogfood workers as input — no
  worker re-derived the delta, re-ran `git diff`, or re-classified risk. The
  old approach (one giant prompt driving the whole dogfood serially) would
  have re-established this context on every lane switch.
- **Risk-selected scope, not a blind full sweep.** 33 of the catalog's 60
  total journeys were selected (10/10 P0 + 17 of 33 P1s + 6 of 17 P2s) —
  driven by the risk mapper's `HIGH` classification and `fallback_used` flag,
  not by running all 60 unconditionally.
- **Deliberately skipped, not silently dropped:** the Design/dashboard lane
  (no Playwright session stood up — recorded as a real coverage gap, not
  treated as passing) and J41 (eBPF — Linux-only, this host is macOS,
  recorded as a genuine platform gap). Both are named in the Skipped/blocked
  table above rather than omitted.
- **3 of 5 available worker slots used**, 2 reserved — consistent with the
  policy's "a narrow patch scope should use 1-3" guidance and the requirement
  to reserve capacity for finding-verification rather than filling all 5
  slots by default.
- **Dedup performed once per candidate finding, not re-litigated.** Both
  AAASM-5832 and AAASM-5833 got exactly one Jira dedup search each before
  independent verification, not a repeated re-search per verification pass.
